
# Embedded Systems I

This log documents the lab sessions for my Embedded Systems I course. Each entry covers a single session: the goal for that day, the hardware setup, the code written to meet it, and a short video showing the circuit actually working on the board.

The sessions build progressively on the RP2350 (Raspberry Pi Pico 2), moving from basic digital outputs (blinking and controlling individual LEDs) to digital inputs (reading push-buttons to reproduce logic gates and control on-board LEDs), and will continue expanding into more advanced peripherals — timers, PWM, analog inputs, communication protocols, and interrupts — as the course progresses.

Beyond the code and results, each entry also includes a short reflection on what went wrong during the session and what I learned from debugging it, along with any open questions I still have going into the next class. The goal is not just to keep a record of finished exercises, but to track my own understanding of embedded systems concepts as it develops throughout the semester.
